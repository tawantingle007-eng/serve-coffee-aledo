About the shop

Made for a slow cup or a quick stop.

Serve is a small coffee shop in Aledo with a warm room, a counter full of espresso gear and retail bags, and seating out front.

Come in to settle down with a book, meet a few friends, or let the kids burn off energy in the outdoor space. If you are in a hurry, grab your order to go.
